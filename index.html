How to play

Choose rock, paper, or scissors. Rock beats scissors, scissors beat paper, and paper beats rock. A tie means you both play again—no point awarded.

Be the first to win three rounds to claim the moonlit match. Each victory reveals a new look in Aurelia’s elegant, fully clothed wardrobe.
