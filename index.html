How to play

Choose rock, paper, or scissors. Rock beats scissors, scissors beat paper, and paper beats rock. A tie means you both play again—no point awarded.

Win five rounds to claim the match. Each win changes one of Aurelia’s five outer wardrobe layers; her opaque satin underdress remains on throughout. Each lost round costs your knight one of his five gear points; ties cost nothing. Reach zero and it’s Game Over.

Tip: you can also use keys 1, 2, and 3.
